**[Mark E. Whiting](/)** is Chief Scientist at [Pareto](https://pareto.ai) and a research fellow at the [CSSLab](https://css.seas.upenn.edu) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu). He was previously a senior scientist at CSSLab, working with [Duncan J. Watts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duncan_J._Watts), in affiliation with [Computer & Information Science](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/index.php) in [Engineering and Applied Science](https://www.seas.upenn.edu) and [Operations, Information and Decisions](https://oid.wharton.upenn.edu) at [Wharton](https://www.wharton.upenn.edu), and a postdoc under [Michael S. Bernstein](https://hci.stanford.edu/msb/) in the [HCI group](https://hci.stanford.edu) in [Computer Science](https://cs.stanford.edu) at [Stanford](http://stanford.edu).
He holds bachelor's and master’s degrees in Industrial Design from [RMIT](https://www.rmit.edu.au) and [KAIST](https://www.kaist.ac.kr/) respectively, and a PhD in Mechanical Engineering from [CMU](https://www.cmu.edu). Find out more about Mark at [whiting.me](/).
